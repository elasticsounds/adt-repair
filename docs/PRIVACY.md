# ADT Repair privacy policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026.

ADT Repair is maintained by [elasticsounds](https://github.com/elasticsounds) and distributed from [this repository](https://github.com/elasticsounds/adt-repair). Privacy and support questions can be raised through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/elasticsounds/adt-repair/issues). Issues are public: do not include private books, personal information, learner responses, credentials or reviewer notes in them.

## Information used

The workflow uses the ADT folder or ZIP, source PDF, page range and any teacher/reviewer notes or annotations that you choose to provide. These materials may contain text, images, names, comments and educational content. Local processing also creates file paths, hashes, inventories, screenshots, issue records, validation results, change logs and reports.

Provide only materials needed for the requested task. The tool does not require access to unrelated files, account credentials or learner histories.

## Purpose

The information is used to compare the ADT with its source, derive book-specific design rules and components, repair requested issues, validate the reader, and produce review reports and delivery artifacts. Input files and copied notes are retained separately from the working repair where the supplied helpers support that separation.

## Processing and recipients

The packaged helper scripts run on the machine or execution environment in which you run Codex. ADT Repair has no publisher-operated processing server, analytics collector or telemetry endpoint. It does not automatically upload your books, notes or reports to its maintainer or GitHub.

Codex may send selected input text, images, prompts and tool results to OpenAI or another model provider configured in your environment. That processing, any model-call logs and provider-side retention are governed by your account, workspace settings and provider policies, not by this plugin. Local helper execution does not make the model operate offline.

Installing dependencies may contact their package registries or browser-download services. Those downloads do not require supplying your textbook or reviewer notes.

If you choose to post a GitHub issue or otherwise share output, the material you submit goes to the destination you select. Public issues can be read by other people. Support submissions are not automatically private.

## Retention

The plugin does not automatically expire or delete local source copies, working files, screenshots, logs or reports. They remain in the locations you choose until you remove them or your execution environment applies its own retention rules. The publisher does not maintain a separate copy of those job files through the plugin.

Codex chat history, model-provider data and any support material you separately submit follow the retention controls of those services. Removing a local repair job does not necessarily remove an existing chat, provider log or public issue.

## Your controls

You can omit reviewer notes, limit the page range, choose audit-only mode, select the output directory and inspect the scripts before running them. You can stop a job and remove its local outputs, uninstall the plugin, and use the applicable account/workspace controls for model processing and chat retention.

The tool does not automatically publish repaired books. Sharing or submitting a book/report to another destination requires a separate user request. When seeking support, use a synthetic or permission-cleared minimal example instead of private source material.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be recorded in this file with an updated effective date. Release notes identify changes to the packaged tool.
